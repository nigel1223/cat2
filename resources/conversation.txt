Interview Notes

I interviewed Marcus, who owns a local independent used car lot in town called Apex Motors. 

Here is what we talked about for his website needs:

1. What he does: 
He sells quality pre-owned cars, trucks, and SUVs. He handles everything himself and prides himself on no-haggle, honest pricing and running every car through a big 150-point inspection so buyers don't get stuck with a lemon.

2. His target audience: 
Mainly local people, families looking for a reliable second car, and students or first-time buyers on a budget who need something dependable for work or school.

3. Important info to display: 
He wants people to know they can trust his dealership right away, so highlighting his inspection promise is huge. He also needs a clean grid area to show off a few featured cars currently on the lot with their prices and miles, and an easy way for people to book a time to come see them.

4. Colors and visual style: 
He wants it to look professional and trustworthy, not flashy. He likes deep navy blue and clean grays/whites. He wanted a clean layout where the text is easy to read.

5. Action visitors should take: 
The main goal is to get people to look at the cars and fill out the contact form to schedule an in-person test drive.
